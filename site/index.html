Masterpiece

Pipe organ sample player for Hauptwerk-format and GrandOrgue sample sets. Draws each organ's own console and plays it from MIDI keyboards.

Consoles

Each organ is drawn from its producer's artwork: drawstops, keyboards, shoes and jamb pages. Thirty-seven sample sets tested, from a nine-register Flentrop to Nancy's four manuals.

Each of these is a separately published sample set, five by Piotr Grabowski and fourteen by Augustine’s Virtual Organs. Full credits are on the attribution page.

Memory

Samples are held at 24 or 16 bits, optionally in mono. Release tails stream from disk. Stops, perspectives and ranks can be left out of a load.

Friesach, 44 stops, 17 GB: 21.6 GB as 32-bit float, 16.2 GB at 24-bit, 4.65 GB at 16-bit streamed, 2.33 GB in mono. Opens in 77 s at full size, 25.9 s streamed, 18.9 s from the sample cache.

Packages

Sets play straight from their RAR archives (RAR 4 and 5, solid and multi-volume) and GrandOrgue .orgue packages. After the first load, the sample cache makes them open as fast as an installed set.

Setup

Wiring

Couplers, octave couplers, reversibles, crescendo and alternate ranks run through the set's own switch network.

Your keyboards

One MIDI channel per manual, with key range, transposition, velocity window, short octaves and debounce. MIDI learn for drawstops, pistons and shoes. One setup serves every organ.

Combinations

Up to 100 generals, divisionals per manual and a 999-frame stepper on every organ. Named combination sets. Favourites on thumb pistons.

Voicing

Level and tuning per rank and per pipe. A and B voicings for comparison.

Loading

Resident format, mono, sample rate, release streaming, sample cache and memory limit, per organ or for all.

Routing

Each rank to one of up to eight output pairs.

Engine

Releases

Chosen by how long the key was held, crossfaded from the sustain.

Wind

Chests, bellows and valves solved as one system. Pressure drops under load; pitch and level follow.

Swell and tremulant

Filtering per swell box. Tremulant per chest.

Temperaments

The organ's own, equal, seven historical, or any Scala file. Pitch in Hz, transposer ±12.

Polyphony

1536 voices. Experimental AVX2, SSE2 and NEON paths, three to five times faster.

Practice

MIDI and audio recording. Metronome.

GrandOrgue

.organ files load with their panels and mix levels.

Hardware

MIDI out to drawstop lamps. Jamb displays over SysEx. Virtual MIDI input on macOS and Linux.

Plugins

VST3, LV2, and AU on macOS.

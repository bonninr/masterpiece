What it is

A player for sampled pipe organs

A sample set is a recording of every pipe of an organ plus a definition of the instrument: its console, stops, couplers, pistons, wind and swell boxes. Masterpiece reads that definition, draws the console, and plays the pipes through the organ's own wiring.

It opens Hauptwerk-format definitions and GrandOrgue .organ files, and plays sets straight from their RAR or .orgue packages. It runs as a standalone application, as VST3 and LV2 plugins, and as an Audio Unit on macOS, on Windows, macOS, Linux, Raspberry Pi, Android and iOS.

Screens

Loading, playing and setting up

Loading

Loads run on their own thread with live progress and a time estimate from the measured rate. Cancel discards the partial organ and keeps the console. A licensed set asks for confirmation once, before its samples load.

Playing

Drawstops, pistons, couplers and shoes work as the builder wired them. A mouse click on a key follows the same path as a MIDI note. The meter reads the final output, after the room and the master level.

Stop list

Every stop grouped by division, for sets spread across many jambs or shipped as text only.

Routing

Up to eight output pairs. Ranks are assigned per organ; unassigned ranks play through the first pair, and all pairs sum in stereo.

Voicing

Level and tuning per rank and per pipe; the two add. A and B hold two complete voicings for comparison. Applied once at note start, so the cost while a note sounds is zero.

Your console

Each MIDI channel plays a manual. Per manual: key range, transpose, velocity window, short octaves, debounce, split keyboards. Learn for drawstops, pistons, shoes, pages and the stepper. One console setup can be the default for every organ.

Favourites and combination sets

Numbered favourites for thumb pistons to open organs. Named combination sets per organ, one per service or recital.

Loading options

Resident format, mono folding, sample rate, release streaming with the share of each release kept in memory, sample cache, memory limit, and which stops, perspectives and ranks to load. Changes apply at once; Save keeps them for this organ or for every organ.

Features

What the engine does

Sample sets

Hauptwerk-format definitions, full and compact. GrandOrgue .organ files, converted on load with their panels. RAR 4 and 5, solid and multi-volume, and .orgue packages read in place. WAV and WavPack samples.

Sound

Attack, crossfaded sustain loop, and a release chosen by how long the key was held. Hermite interpolation. 1536 voices; releases are stolen first.

Wind

Chests, bellows and valves solved as one system. A full registration lowers the pressure, and pitch and level follow it.

Expression

Enclosures filter per box as the shades move. Tremulants modulate level and pitch per pipe.

Memory

24-bit, bit-identical to the files, or 16-bit; optional mono. A 44-stop, 17 GB set: 21.6 GB as 32-bit float, 16.2 GB at 24-bit, 4.65 GB at 16-bit with releases streamed, 2.33 GB in mono.

Streaming

Release tails stream from disk through per-voice ring buffers. Each release keeps its first second, or 25, 50 or 75% of its length, in memory. The status line reports when the disk falls behind.

Loading

A decoded sample cache makes the second load one file read: 77 s at 32-bit float, 25.9 s streamed in mono, 18.9 s from the cache. Stops, perspectives and single ranks can be left out.

Vector engine

AVX2, SSE2 or NEON, chosen per processor: 3 to 5 times faster on 512 voices. Experimental, switched on in Organ settings.

Registration

Couplers, sub- and super-octaves and unison off through the set's own switch network. Up to 100 generals, divisionals per manual, general cancel, crescendo, and a 999-frame stepper.

Tuning

The organ's temperament, equal, seven historical temperaments, or a Scala file. Pitch in Hz, transposer ±12 semitones.

MIDI

All inputs at once. MIDI out lights a console's drawstops; jamb displays run over system exclusive. A virtual MIDI input on macOS and Linux.

Recording

MIDI with stops and shoes, replayable through another registration, and audio alongside. A metronome in the organ's own audio stream. Convolution reverb for dry sets.

Building

CMake and Ninja

cmake --preset dev
cmake --build --preset dev

C++20, CMake 3.22+, Ninja; JUCE and the other libraries are fetched by CMake. CI builds Windows, macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Linux, Raspberry Pi, Android and iOS. Details in the README.

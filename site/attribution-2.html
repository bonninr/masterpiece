Fourteen are the freely published demonstration sets, which include only some of each organ's stops; Giubiasco is the complete set. Each remains the property of its producer and is governed by that producer's terms. None is included with Masterpiece.

Adaptation

What was changed to play them

The sequences were written for a Roland Sound Canvas and required four changes before a sampled organ could play them.

Channel assignment

SCPOP places the manuals on channels 1 and 2 and the pedal on channel 3; the sequences are remapped to the key flow a sampled organ expects.

Pedal compass

Notes below a physical pedalboard's range are folded into it, and the octave doubling used to add weight on the Sound Canvas is removed, since a 32′ or 16′ stop supplies it.

Registration

The system-exclusive messages that selected Sound Canvas voices have no meaning here; registration is drawn on the instrument.

Ties

Where a sequence releases the notes of an arpeggiated chord as the next note arrives and the score ties them, the notes are sustained. The two diminished sevenths in BWV 565 are the principal case.

Score readings were checked against the Mutopia Project edition of BWV 565, typeset in LilyPond and released to the public domain.
